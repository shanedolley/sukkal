Sukkal

Sukkal is a personal assistant that one person runs on their own server. It reads their mail, calendar and trackers, then briefs them on what needs attention.

Read the privacy policy Terms of service

What it does

Sukkal reads mail, calendar events and documents from the Google accounts its owner connects. It builds a daily briefing of what happened overnight and what needs a reply.

It labels mail by customer and project. It drafts replies and leaves each draft for the owner to edit and approve. It keeps a memory of the people, decisions and commitments in that material, and it answers questions from it.

What it never does

Sukkal never sends mail on its own. A message leaves only after the owner approves it.

It never deletes mail, files or facts. It retires or replaces them, so every change can be undone.

Who runs it

Shane Dolley operates Sukkal as an individual, from Queensland, Australia. Each installation has one owner and serves nobody else.

Google accounts

The owner chooses which Google services to connect, and can remove a connection at any time. The privacy policy lists every permission Sukkal asks for and says why.
